Forge Roofing Partners

You have been getting the calls. This is the other side of the table.

Private equity wants to buy your roofing company for one payout. We built the alternative: keep ownership, join a platform of operators, and get paid more than once.

The idea

Consolidation arbitrage, with you on the inside.

Buyers pay a lower multiple for a standalone roofing company and a much higher one for a platform of them. That gap is the whole private equity business. Forge Roofing Partners puts that gap in the operators’ pockets. You roll your company into the platform, keep a stake, run it with our systems, and share in what the platform is worth, not just what your company was worth alone.

Growth engine

Repeatable customer acquisition with the pipeline visible every week. The same play on this site, run inside every platform company.

Cash flow waterfall

Every dollar has a job before it lands. Real profit, not accounting profit.

Operating system

A business that runs without the founder in the middle of it. Scorecards, meeting rhythm, and people who own outcomes.

How you get paid

Three paydays instead of one.

1

Distributions

Once the platform reaches institutional scale, operators share in distributions along the way.

2

Value creation

When a new company joins, the platform is revalued at platform multiples. Everyone already inside benefits.

3

The exit

Exit proceeds tied to what your company contributed to the platform, at the platform’s multiple.

A company making a million dollars of profit might sell alone for a few million. Inside a scaled platform it can be worth several times that. The numbers are illustrative. The conversation is real.

Who it is for

Real companies. Real profit. Real ambition.

  • A real business with solid revenue
  • Genuine profitability, not just on paper
  • Operational excellence, or the willingness to run our systems to get there
  • The ambition to build something larger than a standalone shop
