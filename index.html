Why it works

Everyone is fighting over the 4 percent. The money is on the other 96.

The average low-slope commercial roof lasts about 25 years, so about 4 roofs in every 100 are up for replacement in any given year. Bid rooms, storm chasing, and ads all compete for those same 4 roofs. The other 96 are somewhere in the middle of their life. The membrane is fine. The caulk, the flashings, and the penetrations are not. Those roofs need a little love, the owner does not know it yet, and nobody is calling them.

We would rather have the 96. This is how you find them.

4 roofs everyone bids on96 roofs that need repairs and have no roofer

We can see the problem before the owner knows.

No other trade gets this. From the air you can see ponding, patches, discoloration, and units that were not there the last time the plane flew over. The picture does not need to be from this week. It only needs to be true enough to make the call.

It is not a marketing problem.

There is no Google search for "my roof is quietly failing." You cannot run an ad for a problem the owner does not know about. So we stop waiting for demand and go to the roof.

We bring our own urgency.

After a hail storm, selling is order taking because the owner knows something happened. The ugly roof photo does the same job on a clear day. It is the reason you are calling.

How it works

Three steps. Nothing is spent until you say yes to a roof.

Your reps do this every morning in Ugly Roof Intelligence, the software we built for it. It takes about twenty minutes to load a day of calls.

1

Lasso a neighborhood. Free.

Open the map, press the lasso, and tap the corners of an area you know. Every commercial roof inside lands in your Queue with its storm history already attached.

2

Approve the roofs worth a call.

Swipe right to approve, left to pass. An approval buys the base layer on that roof: the businesses in the building with phone numbers, the owner on the county record, hail and wind with dates, the roof age, the pictures, and the parcel.

3

Call with the evidence in hand.

On the roofs you are going to call, open the permit history or the person behind the owner company for a credit each, log every reach-out, and book the inspection. The dashboard keeps score for the whole team.

What the rep says on the first call

“Hey, Cody here with the roofing company up the road on 67th Street. We have some projects going on in the area, so we have been looking at the roofs around you on satellite. I have some photos I need to get to somebody at your company. If the roof leaked into the lobby tomorrow, who would you call?”

That is the whole first touch. The front desk gives up the name. The brief goes to that person. The inspection gets booked off the brief. You are not cold calling. You are delivering photos.
On the roof

Get on the roof. Fix the small things. Become their roofer.

We do not walk a roof to sell a replacement. We walk it to find out where it is in its life, price the small issues while they are small, and attach a service agreement. The replacement comes to us when it comes, because we are already their roofer.

First visit$5,000 to $8,000

Map the roof, number the bad squares from critical to low, and price each one. The owner picks what fits the budget. The average first ticket across our companies is $7,200 at 65 to 80 percent gross margin.

Every year$2,000 to $4,000 per roof

Walk it twice a year on a service agreement. Update the moisture map and add the new items to the list. You are the person managing their asset now.

Plain answers

Common questions

What happens right after I pay?
You land on a short welcome: where you work, who is on your team, and on Territory, which area is yours. Then the map opens. Most people lasso their first neighborhood that same night and their reps have roofs to call in the morning. The welcome email has the next live training on it.
Is the training really on every door?
Yes. The weekly live sales training with Cody comes with Hunt, Crew, and Territory. The join button and the recordings sit on your dashboard. The doors differ on volume and on the territory, not on the training.
Why am I billed every 28 days instead of monthly?
Our data vendors refresh on a 28 day cycle and your pipeline is re-checked on that same cycle for storms, permits, and the warranty clock. We bill on the cycle the data runs on, so every period you pay for is a period of fresh data.
Do unused approvals roll over?
No. Approvals and included credits refresh every 28 days and expire unused. The system is built for consistent prospecting, not data hoarding. Roofs you approved stay in your pipeline, locks run their full period, every fact you opened stays open, and every report stays yours. Packs of approvals and packs of credits are separate and never expire.
What is an approval?
Lassoing an area is free. Every roof inside lands in your Queue with its storm history. When you approve one, it moves into your pipeline with the base layer: the business and its phone, every other business in the building, the pictures, the parcel, the owner on the county record, the roof age, and the hail and wind. That is one approval.
What is a credit?
A dollar. On a roof page, the permit history is 1 credit, the person behind the owner company and their line is 1 credit, and a Property Intelligence Report is 10. Each is paid once per roof for your whole team. Your plan includes a pile every period, and you can buy more in packs that never expire.
What is a territory?
A protected area, sized to about 7,000 to 10,000 commercial businesses. One roofer per territory. While you hold it, every commercial roof inside is blacked out on every other roofer’s map, every commercial roof inside it is loaded into your Queue with its storm history, and every roof you add anywhere locks to you for 90 days.
Is the imagery current?
Not always, and it does not need to be. The picture is the reason for the call, not the diagnosis. If the owner tells you the roof was replaced last year, you have just learned the roof has a warranty and probably nobody maintaining it. That is a service agreement conversation.
Does it grade the roof for me?
No. The person reads the picture. We put the close-up in front of you with the facts beside it. A roofer with ten seconds and a satellite photo makes a better call than any score.
Is there a contract or a setup fee?
No. You pay for the period you are in. Cancel any time from the Settings page and your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Your pipeline, your notes, and your reports stay yours. Growth Partner is the one exception: it is a thirteen-week engagement, because that is how long it takes to fill a calendar and keep it full.
